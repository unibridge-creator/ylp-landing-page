Frequently Asked Questions

Who issues the teaching permit?

The teaching permit is issued by the Ministry of Higher Education (MOHE).

Will I be bound to Innovative University College after completing this course?

No automatic bond. Opportunities are subject to performance, availability and selection process.

Is this program conducted online or in person?

The program is conducted online.

On which days are the classes held?

Classes are held during weekends.

Can I apply to be a lecturer at IUC?

Yes, selected candidates may be considered for lecturing opportunities, subject to performance and university requirements.

What if I am working and cannot attend weekdays?

This program is designed with weekend classes to support working adults.
